    FAQ

    Questions before booking?

    Do I have to pay before the cleaning?

    Not necessarily. Payment arrangements can be agreed depending on the final booking. Payment after service may be available.

    Can I book only one small service?

    Yes. Carpet Cleaning, Sofa Cleaning, Oven Cleaning, Fridge-Freezer Cleaning, cupboards, internal windows and safely reachable external windows can be requested separately.

    Can I send photos of the property?

    Yes. The booking form allows optional property photos so the cleaning requirements can be understood more clearly.

    Is the price shown on the estimator final?

    No. The estimator gives a likely price range. The final quote can depend on the property's condition, size, access and exact work.

    Do you clean external windows?

    External window cleaning can be requested where the windows are safely reachable. Unsafe or specialist access is not included.

    How quickly can I get a cleaning service?

    Availability depends on the requested date, location and service. The website includes a 48-hour service guarantee statement, subject to the final booking arrangement.
